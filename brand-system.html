01 — Logo Options

Four Marks, One Pulse

Every option is built from the same idea: the crossbar of the H is a heartbeat line. Pick the primary lockup, keep the monogram as your app icon and social avatar, and use the badge for merch and stamps.

01

The Stack

Primary lockup. Vertical, confident, reads at a glance on a hoodie back or a landing-page hero.

02

Pulse Mark

The monogram, on its own. App icon, favicon, watermark, profile picture. This is the shape people remember.

03

The Lockup

Horizontal, for website headers, email signatures, and anywhere width beats height.

04

The Crest

Emblem for merch, race stickers, and stamps. Swap the town line for a race or a cohort name.

02 — Color

Warm Energy, Cold Data

Ignite carries the effort and every call to action. Carbon is the grind. Bone is the human warmth that keeps it from feeling like a gym-equipment catalog. Frost is reserved for numbers, splits, and the race-time estimator.

Ignite

#FF4127
CTAs, the pulse line, one accent per screen. Large sizes only.

Carbon

#14171A
Primary text and dark backgrounds. The anchor.

Bone

#F6F1E9
Light backgrounds and reversed text. The warm human tone.

Steel

#5B6670
Secondary text, captions, hairlines, muted UI.

Frost

#38C0E0
Data only: times, splits, charts, the estimator widget.
